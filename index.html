What is CapraIQ?

CapraIQ is a governed decision system for ecommerce growth. Agents investigate and propose; deterministic code computes every number. A human always decides.

  • Contribution profit, not ROAS. Net revenue minus COGS, shipping, payment fees and ad spend. Missing costs are flagged, never estimated. Ad-platform ROAS is attribution evidence only.
  • Evidence grades A-F. A = reconciled to store totals, sourced costs, signal persisted 7+ days. B = minor gaps. C = attribution-only. D = mismatch or contradicted. E = under 3 days. F = missing critical data.
  • Max 5 decisions per brief, ranked, each with expected impact and "what would prove this wrong".
  • Authority ladder: observe < draft < bounded < approval-required < prohibited.
  • Read-only v1: every external write is blocked by the policy engine.

Metric definitions

  • net_revenue v1: gross - discounts - refunds; refunds on refund day; excludes tax and shipping charged. Source: store platform.
  • contribution_profit v1: net_revenue - COGS - shipping cost - payment fees - ad spend; fixed costs excluded; missing costs flagged not estimated.

  • refund_rate v1: refunds / gross on the same period (cash basis).
  • cohort_ltv v1: cohort by first-order month; LTV_N = mean net revenue within N days of first order over customers observed >= N days; observed data only.

Architecture, agents and policy engine

sample data adapter -> ingest -> tenant-scoped store
  -> metrics engine / anomaly rules / data-quality checks
  -> agents propose (Data Steward, Profit Analyst, Ads Auditor, Lifecycle [phase 2 stub])
  -> Skeptic tries to disprove; only role that sets grades
  -> Chief of Staff dedupes, ranks, keeps top 5, logs to ledger, renders brief
  • Per-client isolation: every data call is scoped to a tenant; no cross-client sharing.
  • Ledger: append-only, SHA-256 hash-chained record of decisions. The demo's chain verified OK (chain_ok=True; 3 and 5 entries for the two tenants).
  • Policy engine: default-deny, role clearances, action registry, full audit log.
